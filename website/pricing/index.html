FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Why is the AI budget measured in weekly dollars instead of monthly message counts?

Different prompts and models consume vastly different amounts of compute. A weekly dollar pool ($0.50/wk on Free, $5.00/wk on Pro) lets you see exact token costs in real time and ensures a single heavy day never locks you out for an entire month — your pool refreshes every Monday at 00:00 UTC.

What happens if I hit my $5.00 weekly pool on Pro before Monday?

You are never locked out on Pro. Once your $5.00 weekly pool is spent, chat and agent requests automatically fall back to our high-speed Auto model at no extra charge until Monday 00:00 UTC.

Do ghost-text Tab completions consume my weekly dollar pool?

No. Tab completions never draw from your weekly dollar pool. Free accounts receive 2,000 completions per UTC calendar month, and Pro accounts get unlimited Tab completions.

How do I cancel or manage my Pro subscription?

Open Settings → Account inside the Synkro desktop app or visit synkro.com/account/billing to launch the customer billing portal and cancel anytime with one click.
